System Guide & Instructions

How to manage buckets, tasks, and Aura AI commands during your evaluation

💰 Managing Money Buckets via Aura Chat

Creating a Bucket

Type natural text commands in Aura Chat like "Create bucket Vacation for 15000" to lock away target savings instantly.

Updating a Bucket

Send update commands through the chat interface like "Update Vacation bucket to 20000" to adjust allocations on the fly.

Deleting a Bucket

Remove targets using text prompts like "Delete Vacation bucket", triggering the guardrails to safely reallocate funds back to your daily spendable balance.
